  • I work on mathematical models of continuous media, with a focus on constitutive modeling from non-equilibrium thermodynamics. My current project is a thermodynamically consistent model of blood as a thixotropic, viscoelastic fluid. My undergraduate thesis studied the diffraction of elastic waves in couple-stress media, and in 2025 I was a research intern in the Department of Mathematics at Purdue University, working with Prof. Yuan Gao on weak KAM solutions of Hamilton–Jacobi equations. I am preparing to start a Ph.D. in mathematics, with a long-term interest in mathematical physics and quantum field theory.

Me preparo para comenzar un doctorado en matemáticas, con un interés de largo plazo en la física matemática y la teoría cuántica de campos.
